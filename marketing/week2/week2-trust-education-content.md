# Week 2 Trust + Education Content Set

Primary goal: increase invite sends per view by reducing trust friction and making first invites feel low-risk.

## Audience

- Roommates and friend groups already splitting expenses
- Users who hesitate to invite because they fear awkwardness or confusion
- Android-first users looking for a simple, transparent workflow

## Value Props To Repeat

- Fast: record once, balances stay clear
- Fair: equal, exact, or percentage split options
- Transparent: everyone can see what changed and what is owed

## 72-Hour Readout Plan

Primary metric:
- Invite sends per view

Secondary metrics:
- CTR from post/reel/video to Play Store
- Installs from campaign-tagged links
- First group created within 24 hours of install

Cadence:
- T+24h: pull top-line per asset (views, clicks, invite sends)
- T+48h: compare CTA variant A vs B by channel
- T+72h: identify winning hook + winning CTA and publish recommendation

Decision rules:
- Keep the highest invite-sends-per-view CTA for next cycle
- Keep the top hook structure and rewrite only the opening line
- Drop any asset with high views but low invite behavior unless comment signals show intent mismatch
